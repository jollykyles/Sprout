⭐ Set up Sprout Premium
1

What premium gets you

Questions about the video your child just watched — real comprehension. The built-in small AI can't do this reliably; premium uses Anthropic's Claude Haiku instead.

Cost: about $0.003 per video question. At 5 videos a day with 2 questions each, that's roughly $0.90/month. You pay Anthropic directly — Sprout takes nothing.

2

Create an Anthropic account

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up (email is fine).
  2. Verify your email if asked.
3

Add a small amount of credit

  1. In the Anthropic console, open Billing (usually under Settings).
  2. Add a card and buy $5 in credit — that lasts a typical family for months.

You'll only get charged for the questions Sprout asks. Set a monthly spend limit in the console if you want a hard cap.

4

Create an API key

  1. In the Anthropic console, open API Keys.
  2. Click Create Key. Name it Sprout.
  3. Copy the key — it starts with sk-ant-. You'll only see it once.
5

Paste your key into Sprout

The key is saved only on this computer, in the extension's local storage. It never leaves your browser except to talk to Anthropic.

✓

Premium is on 🎉

Sprout will now ask comprehension questions about the video your child watched. Everything else stays on-device.
